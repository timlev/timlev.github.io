Rainbow Letter Builder

Every stroke gets the next rainbow color, and the colors keep going from one letter to the next (A, a, B, b ...). Start at the numbered dot and follow the arrow. When it says ↑ Pick up your pencil, lift it before the next stroke. Directions follow the CORE letter formation scripts (Least column).

  1. Red
  2. Orange
  3. Yellow
  4. Green
  5. Blue
  6. Indigo
  7. Violet

Steps

Each card replays one step. Tap a card to show that step on the big board.

Keyboard: Space plays the whole letter, arrow keys move step by step.
